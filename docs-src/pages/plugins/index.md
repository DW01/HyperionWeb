## Turning plugins on and off

Open **Plugins...** from the editor's settings menu. Each plugin is listed with its version, description and the plugins it depends on. Tick the ones you want.

When a plugin is off:

- its systems, import steps and editor tools don't load, and its C# assemblies aren't used.
- anything it added to your project's data is kept to prevent losing your data. If you choose to re-enable the plugin, your data will be available again.
